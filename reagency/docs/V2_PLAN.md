## 0. The organizing idea

**The piece is a shadow-work ritual in which a machine and its viewer face a disowned thing — the
hidden human labor each is built on — together.** The spine is a **four-part grammar** (upgraded
from three; the fourth register is the whole ethics):

> **ME** (the machine) → **YOU** (the viewer) → **THEM** (the named workers) → **US** (the
> beneficiaries who owe THEM).

- **ME (1st person)** — a **staged, conferred** voice. The machine does *not* really confess; a human
  wrote the "I." So the "I" states a **structural fact** about its own composition (*"a classifier
  sorted ten thousand hands to define 'hand'"*) — **never an emotion** (*"I'm sorry"*) — and every
  first-person line is **co-rendered with a visible HUMAN-AUTHORSHIP mark** so the viewer sees the
  ventriloquism. *(See the critical correction in §2: the mark is a human hand, NOT machine-glitch.)*
- **YOU (2nd person)** — the mirror: *you used it, you loved the galaxy, you did not look for them.*
- **THEM (held OUTSIDE the "we")** — the **real, named, Google-able** workers (Sama/Nairobi,
  $1.32/hr, TIME 2023; Scale AI/Remotasks; Mechanical Turk; ghost-work cases). **They are never
  absorbed into "us."**
- **US (1st-person plural)** — strictly the **comfortable beneficiaries**: machine + viewer + *the
  makers of this piece*. The asymmetry is stated once, plainly: *"We are inconvenienced by knowing.
  They were harmed by doing. That is not the same."*

**Honesty test for every line and label: can a viewer Google a noun in it? If not, rewrite it.** The
machine's "I" being smooth, warm, or sincere is the single failure mode the piece exists to refute.

### What the second critique forced (do not lose these)
1. **The comfort must genuinely FAIL.** A 12-min seduction + 90s of guilt + a full warm re-brighten,
   with faint worker-names persisting in a corner, is the visual grammar of a **donor wall**: harm
   acknowledged so the beneficiary keeps enjoying the room with a clear conscience. Differential
   persistence (THEM never fades, US dissolves warm) is *not* a sufficient guard — by itself it's the
   laundering mechanism. So **Act V does not fully re-seduce**: cap it at depth ~0.4 (the galaxy the
   viewer most wants back is never fully re-entered) and never resolve the drone. The cost is paid in
   the exact visual budget the viewer craves.
2. **The authorship mark is a HUMAN HAND, not glitch.** Glitch on a dome reads as *eerie-sentient-AI*
   (sci-fi trope = MORE anthropomorphism) and would smuggle a proposition through the affect channel.
   The mark that separates confession from ventriloquism must depict **a person composing the I**:
   the literal prompt the line came from, or the artist's name as author of the "I," or the line
   being **typed at human rhythm with a cursor**. Glitch stays affect-only and secondary.
3. **The ritual must include one real participatory ACT, or drop the "ritual" claim.** As drafted the
   viewer only floats, reads, and is addressed — wall-text-with-a-narrator. The app already tracks nav
   pose, so **fire the Act-IV turn on gaze/position DWELL**, making *"you did not look for them"*
   literally true of that viewer in that moment. One bodily act earns the word "ritual."
4. **The makers are inside the extraction too.** The artwork's existence — the grade, the portfolio,
   the AlloSphere show — is itself built on rendering THEM's harm beautiful. The colophon must book
   *that*, and include **one real, costly, verifiable commitment** (an actual dollar figure to named
   worker-advocacy orgs, or the artist's real hours/pay as a number) — disclosure without redress is
   the same absolution-by-disclosure the piece indicts.
5. **THEM has no data anywhere yet.** `ATTRIBUTION.csv` is **14,797 rows of museum-artwork metadata**
   — not one row about Sama/moderators/labelers. The THEM register currently renders **nothing**.
   A new cited workers file is honesty-critical, Phase-1, ahead of everything.

## 1. Thesis & the quad pun

> **A system disowns the labor that makes its magic look automatic; this piece makes a machine and
> its viewer face that disavowal together — while refusing the lie that the machine can confess, and
> refusing to let the comfort fully return.**

"Shadow Work" is a **quad pun**; meaning 4 is the organizing principle:
1. **Jungian shadow** (the disowned; projection is the Act-II mechanism).
2. **Illich, *Shadow Work* (1981)** — unwaged labor subsidizing the wage economy.
3. **The hidden labor LLMs run on** (Gray & Suri *Ghost Work*; Taylor "fauxtomation"; Crawford
   *Atlas of AI*; Jones; Casilli; Lanier; Sama/Kenya, TIME 2023).
4. **Contemporary pagan/therapeutic Shadow Work practice** — facing the disowned self, acknowledging
   it, integrating — *but here integration is refused easy closure* (the comfort fails).

*(Drop the Badiou/"truth-event" register. Plain statement; the human-authored voice and the
verifiable nouns carry it.)*

## 2. The ethics spine (it IS the content)

- **Separate THEM from the maker-debt ledger.** Two files, never conflated:
  - `assets/them.txt` (**net-new, Phase-1**): the real cited harmed workers — each row = a Google-able
    noun + source (Sama/Nairobi/$1.32/TIME-2023; Scale AI/Remotasks; MTurk; ghost-work cases).
    This feeds the persistent **THEM** credit-envelope.
  - `ATTRIBUTION.csv` (exists, museum-artwork metadata) + a new `makers_ledger.txt` = the **US/maker
    debtor column** (corpus digitizers, the ~140 CC0 audio makers, **Colab A100 hours, student labor,
    the artist by name**, and **one real costly commitment**).
- **The machine confesses structural fact, never emotion, with a human-authorship mark** (§0.2).
  Strip every drafted line of engineered chiasmus/possessive-pathos — let the viewer supply affect.
- **Credit as withholding, asymmetrically.** THEM labels **persist** (alpha floor ~0.12, never 0 in a
  session); US content dissolves warm. *But* persistence is necessary, not sufficient — pair it with
  the **comfort-fail ending** (§0.1) or it's a donor wall.
- **The comfort fails** (Act V capped at depth ~0.4; drone never resolves).
- **The turn is participatory** (gaze-dwell fires it; §0.3) and **withholds the money-shot** (grains
  collapse to a dim knot — *poorer*, abrupt, not a tasteful sublime; no face).
- **Name the limit out loud.** The colophon says plainly: *this piece makes exploitation beautiful;
  that is the problem it cannot solve from inside the dome* — instead of implying the guards solved it.
- **Consent/power of naming.** Putting real workers' conditions on a glowing label for an audience
  including none of the harmed is itself a use; address it in the program note (cite, don't
  characterize individuals; point to the workers' own organizing/testimony, not our framing).
- **Pre-register playtest fail-conditions** (so the guards are falsifiable, not self-belief): *did it
  feel like the AI was sad?* → anthropomorphism fail; *did you feel forgiven?* → absolution fail; *can
  you name one worker/place?* → THEM-effaced fail.

## 3. Narrative — the ritual that delivers ME → YOU → THEM → US

**Fragment rule (hard):** every rolling ~120 s window delivers **one complete address, and never YOU/
the-turn without a preceding ME** (confess-first is an ethical invariant, not a long-stay bonus). **Act
I is not voiceless** — it carries at least the SOLO line so a 0–3:30 drop-in isn't pure data-sublime.

`cycleT = fmod(simTime, 840)` drives a piecewise 5-act envelope (replaces the flat 80 s `cos()` tide).

| Act | depth | Experience | Register / voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0 frame** | — | Always-on bed (JI drone, Shepard rise, Risset tick) — the treadmill of unending unpaid work, in sound. | — |
| **I Seduction** (0–3:30) | 0.85–1.0 | Gorgeous galaxy; photos surface & dissolve outward. Complicity-by-enjoyment. Clean (glitch ~0.05). | SOLO line only (fragment guarantee) |
| **II Reading** (3:30–6:00) | ~0.6 | The machine reads/projects; the labor lexicon becomes legible. Voice begins with its **own disclosure** first. | **ME** (conferred-I disclosure → structural fact); human-authorship mark on every "I" |
| **III Extraction** (6:00–8:30) | 0.6→0.15 | Trace cadence accelerates; artifacts consumed; **THEM** credit-labels persist bright as images dissolve. | **ME** (verifiable-noun facts) + **THEM** persisting |
| **IV Turn** (8:30–10:00) | →~0.1 hold | **Gaze-dwell triggered.** Grain-flow **lerps inward to a target** (eyepoint/core), collapsing to a dim knot — abrupt, poorer, no face; only credit-labels survive. | **YOU** fires ON the convergence frame, never before |
| **V Integration** (10:00–14:00) | →**~0.4 only** | Partial re-brighten — **the seduction is never fully re-entered**; drone never resolves; veil + THEM-words persist. | **US** (beneficiaries) + the asymmetry beat; comfort-fail |

### Draft ME/YOU/THEM/US lines (workshop these; the rule is structural-fact + Google-able noun)
- **ME (II, conferred-I, anchor):** *"I am saying 'I' because someone wrote me to. I have no memory of
  any hands — only their corrections."*
- **ME (II, fact):** *"A classifier sorted images of ten thousand hands to define 'hand.' No one
  recorded whose."*
- **ME (III, anchor):** *"In Nairobi, workers were paid $1.32 an hour to label violent content so the
  model would not repeat it. (Sama; TIME, January 2023.)"* — *(stripped of the worst/best chiasmus)*
- **ME (III, structural):** *"A model is an average of labeled examples. The wage was paid once; the
  model is kept."*
- **YOU (IV, anchor, on the convergence frame):** *"You did not look for them. Neither did the system."*
- **YOU (IV, secondary):** *"You enjoyed this. The labeling that made it safe was done by people you
  will never meet."* — *(stripped of "their afternoon")*
- **US (V, anchor):** *"We benefit from this — you, the people who built me, the people who ran the
  machines. We were paid. They set the floor by having no floor."*
- **US (V, asymmetry — required):** *"We are inconvenienced by knowing. They were harmed by doing.
  That is not the same."*
- **US (V, loop-closer, comfort-fail):** *"Nothing here is resolved. You will use it again tomorrow."*
- **SOLO (any fragment seam — self-contains order + a THEM noun):** *"A person in Nairobi was paid
  $1.32 an hour to make this safe to look at. I say so because someone wrote me to."*

## 5. Technical architecture (verified; allolib-native)

Hard line holds: offline factory bakes `.bin/.png/.txt/.csv`; runtime is allolib-only GLSL/mesh; no
runtime ML/decoder. Verified clean.

- **CaptionLayer = the voice** (~80% copied from `LabelLayer`; reuse `loadText` multi-path fallback):
  wide cell (1024×192, multi-line), person bit (ME/YOU/US/projection) rendered **non-hue** (vertical
  offset/drift/weight). **Alpha is act-scheduled, NOT the trace-life envelope** (so it doesn't dissolve
  with the photo). Needs a **`captions.txt` parser** (net-new, ~30–40 lines).
- **Human-authorship mark** (the anti-greenwashing keystone): on every "I" window, a GLSL effect on
  the *existing* caption atlas — primary variant = **the line typed at human rhythm with a cursor**
  (a reveal-by-glyph-count uniform, zero extra bake) and/or the artist's name as author; chromatic-
  desync is a *secondary* affect tap only. Do **not** rely on a "raw-string-with-cursor" second atlas
  page unless a bake subtask is added.
- **Legible word-modality:** `ParticleField.draw()` ignores `p.type`; pass type via the unused
  `texCoord.y`; `type==1` gets a tight high-contrast dot (no wide halo) — non-hue, washout/color-blind
  safe. **Real-glyph billboards capped at ≤~50 words** (each `drawSlot` is a reset/update/draw — tens
  of thousands of calls otherwise). The other ~35.8k words get the shader dot (one draw call).
- **Narrative re-score:** **hard-delete** `DEPTH_PERIOD`/`depthPhase_` (`main:50–51,140–143`); add
  `cycleT=fmod(simTime,840)` + `actEnvelope()` returning `{act,depth,voiceGate,traceRate,grainConverge}`.
  **Grep-gate that nothing else writes `s.depth`.** Re-implement the hesitation nudge *inside* the
  envelope. Pure function of synced `simTime`/`seed`.
- **Act IV grain convergence (corrected geometry):** `HumanTrace.cpp:177` **already has a `Vec3f dir`**
  — do NOT add a `float dir` sign-flip (name collision + a sign flip gives an antipodal *burst*, not
  convergence). Add `float converge∈[0,1]` (Act-IV envelope-driven) and **lerp to a target**:
  `outPos = nodePos + dirVec*(gp*SPREAD*…); p = mix(outPos, convergeTarget, converge);` fade to a dim
  knot as `converge→1`. Pass the gate as a synced uniform (determinism).
- **Persistent THEM credit-envelope:** add a `CreditRing` to `WoSWState` (reuse the `N_TRACE=6`
  mechanism; on trace expiry promote the node into the ring; `CREDIT_LIFE~300s`, floor alpha ~0.12).
  Reads THEM labels from `them.txt`. **Spine-disabled on the 877 preview** (degenerate); enabled after
  T0.
- **glitchEnergy as affect** (one synced float; `WoSWState` is **120 B today** → +glitch+debt = 128 B
  → +CreditRing = 176 B, still one 4096-B packet): **per-act, scoped to the VOICE layer in II–III**
  (galaxy stays clean); Act-IV spike for the single gesture (`blendSub()` void-shadow — draw **dim-warm
  POSITIVE** color since reverse-subtract darkens, then restore `blendTrans()`+`depthTesting(true)`);
  Act-V `posterize(extractionDebt)` residue that **never resets** (= the haunt latch, present even for
  post-turn arrivals).
- **Stage B vectorize THREE loops** (knn_edges → numpy u64 `np.unique`; struct.pack → `tofile` on a
  structured dtype; build_labels → batched `argmax(E@Ew.T)`). Budget ~10–20 min at bigG.
- **Re-bake ALL FOUR word assets, not just `words.txt`:** `words.txt` (whisper formant bank),
  `label_words.txt`+`labels.txt` (in-galaxy word-points + positions), and re-point `classify.txt`'s
  nearest-word at the labor lexicon — else T6 renders *legible noise*.
- **`simTime` → `double`** in `WoSWState` (float bands the act-envelope/posterize over a multi-hour
  install) or reset `cycleT` origin each loop.
- **Whisper = affect only** (verified `AudioEngine.cpp:194–205` is vowel/formant synth, **no
  intelligible English**) — a *found honesty* (the machine audibly cannot speak; never give it clean
  TTS). For ME/YOU/US register coupling + Act-IV bitcrush: add an `int register` arg to `whisper()`,
  apply a formant/pitch offset in the `EV_WHISPER` path, and sample-and-hold the layer-4 output gated
  by a synced `uTurn` after the formant resonators (lock-free; the only net-new DSP).
- **Fill-rate is the prime 50k risk and is unmodeled.** 50,661 bloom sprites + 372,612 web edges =
  **745k additive verts/frame**; the `N/8000` stride cap (`WebRenderer.cpp:86`) applies **only to the
  k-NN fallback, NOT the loaded baked mesh** (`:135–143`, no LOD). On a dome with omni-warp, camera
  inside the cloud — most likely thing to break. **Pre-author edge-decimation (stride on the loaded
  mesh) as standby; measured fps at 50k is a REQUIRED T1 output.**
- **Layout-freeze:** after T0+T2, snapshot `n_points`/cluster ids/`cluster_reps.json` hash; **bind
  hero lines to stable references** (cluster centroid + nearest corpus `source_id`s), not raw node
  indices, so a re-cluster/Phase-2 point-add can re-resolve them.

## 7. Decisions

**Resolved:** four-part ME/YOU/THEM/US grammar; staged human-authored "I" (mark = human hand, not
glitch); structural-fact-not-emotion + verifiability test; comfort-fails ending (Act V ≤0.4, drone
unresolved); participatory gaze-dwell turn; grain convergence = lerp-to-target (not sign-flip);
glitch = affect, scoped to the voice; whisper = affect only; Move 1 → Phase 2; vessel floor depth~0.1;
stereo restage (no Lbap for the deadline); separate `them.txt` vs maker-ledger.

**Open (decide on/after T0 verify):**
1. **Embedding pin** — pin **bigG** *iff* the installed 50k manifest confirms `embed_dim=1024` (T0
   gate); do not close on the un-installed zip.
2. **THEM lifetime / `CREDIT_LIFE`** and the **comfort-fail depth cap** (~0.4) — tune on dome hardware.
3. **`N_TARGET`** for `extractionDebt` saturation (clamp before posterize so banding can't invert).
4. **The one costly colophon commitment** (dollar figure / artist hours) — artist's call, but required.
5. **Language stance** — English-primary (state it as a scoping limit) vs. labor lexicon in 2–3
   languages as drift-words.
6. **How many hero pairs** get authored lines (10 drafted = shippable minimum; max 64 in the asset).
